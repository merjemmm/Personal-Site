Lake Erie Analysis in Python

A team and I investigated how nutrient levels in the western basin of Lake Erie have changed over time and whether high nutrient levels correlate with known Harmful Algal Bloom (HAB) events by plotting, cross-examining, and researching physical implications of our data. There was a Kendal's Tau correlation coefficient of 0.44 (a strong relationship) between Total Phosphorous and Nitrtate Nitrite concentrations in the water.

  • Technologies: Python, numpy, matplotlib, Data Analysis

The Status of Electric Vehicles in Washington

Analyzed public records of Vehicles licensed in Washington from Jan 2017 to Sep 2024. Currently, Teslas are the most brand of EV across the state with 4x as many cars as the second most popular brand. A Polynomial Regression model was trained on Jan 2017 to Oct 2023 and tested on Sep 2023 to Sep 2024 to achieve an R-squared of 0.4.
It has an entire working website with detailed inforgraphics and explanations.

  • Technologies: Python, pandas, numpy, plotly, scikit-learn, Github
  • Skills: Machine Leanring, Polynomial Regression
